📜 Community Rules

All players, civilians and departments alike, must follow these rules. Breaking them can mean a warning, a kick, or a permanent ban.

Section 1: General Roleplay

  1. No RDM (Random Death Match): Do not kill or attack players without a valid roleplay reason.
  2. No VDM (Vehicle Death Match): Do not use vehicles to hit or kill players without a valid roleplay reason.
  3. No FRP (Fail Roleplay): Your actions must be realistic. Stay in character and act as a real person would.
  4. No Metagaming: Do not use out-of-character information, such as Discord, streams, or other players’ voice chat, in roleplay.
  5. No Powergaming: Do not force actions on other players or make them impossible to respond to.
  6. No Combat Logging: Do not leave the game during an active scene or conflict.
  7. No LTAP (Leaving to Avoid Punishment): Leaving to avoid a punishment results in a ban, up to permanent for moderation-related offenses.
  8. No RTAP (Respawning to Avoid Punishment): Respawning to escape a scene or arrest is typically a warning.
  9. New Life Rule (NLR): After you die, you forget how you died and the events leading up to it, and you may not return to that scene for 10 minutes.
  10. Safe Zones: A safe zone is any place where players spawn: civilian spawn, River City Police Department, Liberty County Sheriff’s Office, River City Fire Rescue, Springfield Fire Department, Liberty State Troopers, the hospital, and any other spawn point. Killing someone in a safe zone is a warning the first time and a kick the second time. This rule replaces the normal RDM penalty in these locations.

Section 2: Driving and Vehicles

  1. Drive realistically. No flying over cars, driving on sidewalks, or impossible stunts.
  2. Traffic laws apply. Drive realistically. Minor violations like speeding, rolling stops, and running a few red lights are tolerated in roleplay, as long as it isn’t anything crazy. Just drive like a normal person and don’t be a maniac.
  3. GTA Driving: Driving unrealistically or above 120 mph will result in a warning or a kick. You may only go above 120 mph if you are in an active pursuit with law enforcement.
  4. Running a red light or stop sign here and there is fine, but running several in a row is reckless driving and will be treated as FRP.
  5. No ramming vehicles without a valid reason.
  6. No drifting in traffic or blocking roads for fun.
  7. Do not use emergency lights or sirens unless you are an on-duty department member.
  8. Do not spawn or drive vehicles reserved for a department you are not in.

Section 3: Civilians

  1. Use a realistic name and backstory for your character.
  2. Do not interfere with scenes you aren’t part of.
  3. Criminal activity such as robberies must be fully roleplayed, with realistic reasons and consequences.

Section 4: Voice Channel Requirements

  1. Anyone playing as law enforcement, fire, EMS, or any other emergency service must be in a voice channel while on duty.
  2. Civilians are not required to be in a voice channel while driving around or doing normal activities. You must join voice once you are in an active scene or traffic stop.
  3. If you are pulled over, you are required to join an open Traffic Stop voice channel for the duration of the stop.
  4. If you are stopped by moderation, you are required to join one of the Mod Scene voice channels right away and stay until staff release you.
  5. Do not leave a voice channel during a stop or scene without being released. Refusing to join or leaving early is treated as LTAP.

🚨 RTO & Priority

Two systems that keep scenes organized and fair.

📻 RTO (Radio Transmissions Only)

RTO means radio transmissions only. The RTO voice channel is only for radio traffic: calls, dispatch, status updates, and scene communication.

  • Keep transmissions short and clear.
  • No side conversations, jokes, or talking over radio traffic.
  • Emergency traffic always comes first. Stop talking and let it through.
  • If you want to chat, leave RTO and use another channel.

🚨 The Priority System

Some actions draw mass attention from law enforcement. If you are going to do one of these, you need priority first.

  • Bank robberies
  • Hostage situations
  • Kidnapping
  • Killing law enforcement

You do not need priority to run from the police. Anything that will draw mass law enforcement attention does need priority.

✅ How Priority Works

  • Ask for priority before you begin, not after.
  • Only one priority can be active at a time.
  • Not sure whether something needs priority? Ask staff before you start.
